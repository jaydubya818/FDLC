# Factory Control Boundary

## What We're Building

Make one architectural distinction unmistakable across FDLC.ai: models, agents, skills, and adapters are replaceable intelligence supply; identity, authority, state, policy, evidence, decisions, and outcome history form the durable factory control system. Add six practical operating shifts to the Framework, a concrete control-boundary model to Architecture, and a compact homepage summary.

## Why This Approach

The supplied reference validates most of FDLC’s current substance, so a new taxonomy page would repeat material and lengthen the journey. Integrating the missing distinction into the canonical pages improves comprehension without borrowing vendor-specific framing or visual language.

## Key Decisions

- Keep the existing six-area FDLC architecture as the source of truth.
- Describe models and agents as variable capability supply, not as the factory.
- Make the Harness and Trust responsibilities visible as a durable control boundary.
- Treat verification capacity, evidence, and governed promotion as operating-system concerns.
- Do not add vendor logos, workforce claims, or competitive-moat language.
- Preserve the site’s editorial field-manual aesthetic and existing navigation model.
