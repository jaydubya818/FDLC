## Selections

| Source date | Finding | What the evidence supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nov 28 | Softwarefabrik 0.63.0 adds an opt-in network allowlist for agent containers. | The publisher describes a hostname proxy, an audit record for denied connections, and a startup check for prerequisites. Approved services can still transmit data. We verified the dated entry on the publisher’s live changelog. We did not independently test the licensed runtime. [Release notes](https://softwarefabrik.io/en/news.html) |
| Nov 26 | Clay’s Jeff Barg describes the effect of large agent batches on other customers. | In the interview, Barg describes a job per row, throttling, and pausing work when a model provider reaches a limit. He describes fairness across customers as an ongoing problem. The entry is labeled as his perspective. The suggested mixed-workload test is FDLC’s application of it. [Interview and transcript](https://latent.space/p/how-clay-builds-gtm-agents-jeff-barg-head-of-ai/) |
| Nov 25 | Zhang and Long rechecked a skill-selection rule against held-out runs. | Their preprint reanalyzes 7,554 public trials and reports an apparent gain even with shuffled condition labels. The held-out comparison found no statistically significant improvement for that rule. The paper also identifies skills bundled by a harness, which matter when defining a baseline. [Versioned preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33943v1) · [Methods and limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.33943v1) |
| Nov 24 | GhostAPI-V compared migration feedback derived from audited requirements with raw version differences. | Five feedback conditions used the same 192 starting candidates. In eight Python repositories, 148 revised candidates passed hidden tests with the full feedback, compared with 120 using raw version differences. The study leaves open how much of that difference came from the wording, human review, or checks. [Versioned preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33396v1) · [Full study](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33396v1) |
